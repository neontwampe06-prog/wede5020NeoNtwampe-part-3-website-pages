About Clothes For All

Our history, our mission and the people behind our work.

Our History

Clothes For All began in Johannesburg in 2018 after a small group of volunteers saw the same problem repeated in neighbourhoods, schools and community centres: good clothing was being discarded while families were struggling to meet basic needs.

What started with a few donation bags and a shared commitment to dignity has grown into a wider network of Collection Centres and volunteers across South Africa. Today, the organisation works with schools, local partners and community groups to collect, sort, clean and redistribute clothing to families, learners and matriculants who need it most.

Our Mission

To keep usable clothing in circulation and make sure it reaches the people who need it most, helping families and learners move forward with dignity, confidence and practical support.

Our Vision

A South Africa where every person has access to practical support, basic essentials and the confidence to participate fully in everyday life.

Who We Serve

Families and Learners

We support households, school learners and matriculants who are dealing with financial pressure and cannot always afford school or formal wear.

Community Partners

We work with companies, schools, churches and local groups who want to give back through clothing donations and practical support.
