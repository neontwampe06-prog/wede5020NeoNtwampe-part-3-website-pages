About Clothes For All

From one community centre in Johannesburg to a growing network across South Africa — this is our story, and the people behind it.

Our History

Clothes For All was founded in 2018 in response to the urgent need for clothing in underprivileged communities across South Africa. A group of community activists and social workers began the organisation as a small team of volunteers based at a single community centre in Johannesburg.

Over the past eight years, we have established Collection Centres in major cities including Cape Town, Durban and Gqeberha, working alongside community centres, schools and local businesses to organise clothing drives. What started with five founding volunteers has grown into a corps of more than 50 volunteers who collect, sort, clean and distribute clothing to anyone in need — including school learners who cannot afford uniforms, and matriculants preparing for their matric dance.

  • 2018 Founded by five volunteers at a single Johannesburg community centre.
  • 2019–2023 Expanded to Collection Centres in Cape Town, Durban and Gqeberha.
  • 2024–2026 Grew to a network of 50+ volunteers reaching Gauteng, the Eastern Cape and KwaZulu-Natal.

Our Mission

To help meet the demand for unwanted clothing and those who need it, by collecting, sorting and resharing good-quality pre-loved clothing with the underprivileged in South Africa — restoring dignity and confidence to each person we serve.

Our Vision

A South Africa where no one is left undressed or stripped of the dignity and confidence they need to engage fully in society.

Who we serve

Primary Beneficiaries

Underprivileged families, children in need of school uniforms, matriculants who cannot afford formal wear, and communities affected by poverty and homelessness.

Recipients & Volunteers

Family service agencies and local charities that receive donated clothing, and the community members who give their time to collect, sort and pass it on.

Corporate Partners

Companies looking for a meaningful way to fulfil their social responsibility commitments through sponsorship and donation.
